FLOODED COFFEE SHOP

Description
Flooded Coffee Shop is a cafe offering coffee, other beverages, pastries, and light meals.
Its menu includes espresso-based drinks, iced coffee, non-coffee drinks, baked treats, and sandwiches. 
The shop is designed as a welcoming place to enjoy a drink, take a break, or study and work.
 Its tagline is "Code. Coffee. Repeat."

Uses
- Browse the coffee shop's menu gallery and prices.
- Create an account and sign in to place orders.
- Choose items and quantities, then add, update, or remove items from a cart.
- Submit an online order and view or print its receipt.
- Review past orders through the account page.
- Find the shop's contact details and opening hours.

The website lists opening hours as 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday through Sunday.
